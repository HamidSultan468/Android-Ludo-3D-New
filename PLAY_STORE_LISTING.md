## Full Description (max 4000 characters)
```
Ludo 3D brings the timeless board game you grew up with into a beautiful
3D world. Roll the dice, race your tokens around the board, and be the
first to bring all your pieces home!

FEATURES
- Classic Ludo rules everyone knows and loves
- Smooth 3D board and token animations
- Play against the computer or with friends
- Capture opponents, land on safe cells, and race to the finish
- Simple, clean controls - just tap to roll and tap to move

HOW TO PLAY
Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your yard. Move your tokens around the
board, capture opponents who land on the same cell (unless they're on a
safe star cell), and get all 4 of your tokens home first to win!

Download now and enjoy this classic game like never before.
```

## Privacy Policy
Agar app koi personal data collect nahi karti (jo abhi is game mein nahi
karti — sirf PlayerPrefs se local settings save hoti hain), tou bhi Google
Play Console **ek Privacy Policy URL maangta hai**. Simple si ek page bana
kar (jaise "Ye app koi personal data collect ya share nahi karti") kahin
free host (Google Sites, GitHub Pages) par daal kar uska link de sakte hain.
