1. AGE RESTRICTION
This service, and any refund it describes, is available only to users aged 18 and over.

2. DEPOSITS
Deposits are processed instantly through JazzCash/Easypaisa once the gateway confirms
payment. A deposit that fails or is not confirmed by the gateway is never credited to
your wallet - no refund action is needed in that case since no charge was completed
successfully on our side. If a payment was deducted by your provider but never appears
in your wallet, contact support with your transaction reference.

3. WITHDRAWALS
Withdrawal requests are deducted from your wallet balance immediately upon request.
Requests up to Rs. 10,000 are paid out automatically; larger requests require manual
review. If a payout fails after your balance was already deducted, the amount is
automatically refunded to your in-app wallet.

4. GAME OUTCOMES
Entry fees and winnings are determined by verified, server-authoritative gameplay.
Once a match is validated and complete, entry fees and payouts are final and are not
refundable on the basis of dissatisfaction with the outcome.

5. FRAUD / ANTI-CHEAT
If our anti-cheat systems determine that a match involved illegal moves, timing
manipulation, or other cheating, we reserve the right to void the affected
transaction(s), freeze the account under review, and withhold or reverse related
winnings, in accordance with these policies.

6. PLAY RESPONSIBLY
Play within your means. This policy does not create an entitlement to a refund for
losses incurred during normal, fairly-played games.

For a refund inquiry, contact support through the in-app support channel with your
transaction reference.
