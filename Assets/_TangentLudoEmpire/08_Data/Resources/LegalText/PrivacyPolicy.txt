1. AGE RESTRICTION
This service is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data
from anyone under 18.

2. DATA WE COLLECT
- Account identifiers (device/profile id, KYC details required by payment regulation).
- Wallet transaction history (deposits, withdrawals, entry fees, winnings).
- Gameplay telemetry (matches played, dice rolls, moves) for anti-cheat and analytics.
- Crash and diagnostic logs (Firebase Crashlytics) to fix stability issues.

3. HOW WE USE IT
- To operate your wallet and process deposits/withdrawals through licensed payment
  gateways (JazzCash, Easypaisa).
- To detect fraud and cheating (server-authoritative dice, move validation, speed-hack
  detection) and protect the integrity of real-money games.
- To improve the app via aggregated, anonymized analytics.

4. DATA PROTECTION
Local wallet and progression data is encrypted (AES-256) and integrity-checked before
use. We never store your payment credentials - deposits/withdrawals go directly through
the payment provider's own secure gateway.

5. THIRD PARTIES
We use Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics) and licensed payment gateways (JazzCash,
Easypaisa) as data processors. We do not sell your personal data.

6. PLAY RESPONSIBLY
We may use your play patterns to surface responsible-gaming reminders or, in extreme
cases, to pause an account pending a wellness check-in.

7. YOUR RIGHTS
You may request a copy of your data or account deletion through in-app support,
subject to the retention we are legally required to keep for financial records.

For questions, contact support through the in-app support channel.
